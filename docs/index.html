no-stress MA concentration calculator

Web version — runs entirely in your browser, nothing is uploaded anywhere. Same maths as the desktop app, ported from monoamine calculation program.R.

Samples

Columns: SAMPLE_ID, MHPG, NE, DHBA, DOPAC, DA, 5-HIAA, 5-HT, PROTEIN, VOLUME
PROTEIN in mg (Bradford assay), VOLUME in mL (0.1 mL standard, higher for a re-run). Paste tab- or comma-separated text with a header row.

Standards

Columns: STD_ID, MHPG, NE, DHBA, DOPAC, DA, 5-HIAA, 5-HT
Paste tab- or comma-separated text with a header row.

Every fixed number from the R script, editable here. Changes are saved to this browser automatically when you click Apply or Calculate, and persist across visits (nothing leaves your machine).
